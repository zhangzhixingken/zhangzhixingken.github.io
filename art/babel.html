Interactive Installation Oct 2024 — Nov 2025

Language / Translation / Cultural Transmission

BABEL

Zhixing Zhang
Enter the work
01

Thesis

Translation is not a bridge.
It is a material.

We live in a multilingual world, yet no sentence crosses from one language to another unchanged. BABEL makes that instability physical. A spoken sentence travels sequentially through twelve linguistic regions and returns to its language of origin — altered, accumulated, and newly strange.

By staging translation as sound, movement, projection, and printed trace, the work asks what disappears when language is treated as frictionless infrastructure. What is lost is not only semantic accuracy, but a way of locating time, space, uncertainty, identity, and relation.

02

Position

Language after the network

No signal passes through a medium unchanged.

Within conceptual art’s history of treating language as material, BABEL moves the proposition into an infrastructural condition. The work does not place mistranslation at the edge of communication; it makes mutation the central event. APIs, language taxonomies, machine voices, and hardware cease to be invisible tools and become the sculpture’s active agents.

Ⅰ

Linguistic relativity

Language influences habitual attention and how a culture segments the world. Concepts that are precise in one language may only survive as broader approximations in another.

Ⅱ

Technology is situated

Gaps in API coverage across Central Asia, Africa, Oceania, and Indigenous language regions reveal that the supposedly universal platform carries the priorities of its makers.

Ⅲ

The untranslatable

The untranslatable is not a failure of communication. It is the point at which cultural specificity becomes visible.

03

Apparatus

Twelve voices.
One sentence.
A return that is never identical.

  1. 01

    Speak

    A participant speaks into the microphone. Speech recognition detects the language and converts the voice into text.

  2. 02

    Transmit

    The sentence passes through twelve linguistic regions, with a language randomly selected from each region during every interaction.

  3. 03

    Embody

    Machine speech travels through twelve speakers while synchronized servos animate the heads around the ring.

  4. 04

    Return

    The sentence returns to its origin language as a transformed utterance, made perceptible through voice, projection, and paper.

04

Encounters

The audience does not stand outside the system. Each voice initiates a new linguistic route, making every encounter an unrepeatable edition of the work.

05

Evolution

The work is a living system.

Each version changes the conditions through which loss can be heard, seen, and carried away.
